OUR SERVICES
------------

  01  Strategic Advisory
      Shaping how brands and agencies commission and plan content at scale.

      What gets made, in what structure, and with whom. Production reality brought into the creative process while the idea is still open. Rosters, formats and sustainability weighed on creative judgement and market evidence.

      Key focus areas
        • Production Strategy — Commissioning principles, partner frameworks and budget benchmarks.
        • Production Reality Check — Bringing production intelligence into the creative process early, without compromising creativity.
        • Content Planning at Scale — Structuring volume, bundling and capacity across the year.
        • Roster & Director Advisory — Independent recommendations on creative fit, format and budget.
        • Emerging Format Strategy — Production pipelines for social-first, micro drama and AI-generated content.
        • Sustainable Production — Carbon budgeting, supplier selection and production data.
      [Button] Talk strategy

  02  Organisational Setup & Studio Builds
      Building the production capability organisations need, from scratch or from inside.

      What belongs in-house and what stays external, clarified before anyone is hired. In-house departments and agency production units built to stand on their own. Roles, approvals and workflows defined, and led until permanent leadership is in place.

      Key focus areas
        • Capability Assessment — What belongs in-house, what stays external, and why.
        • In-House Studio Build — Brand-side production units built from scratch or from existing teams.
        • Agency Production Setup — Production capability inside agencies, from structure to staffing.
        • Operating Model & Process Design — Roles, approvals and workflows.
        • Post & VFX Capability — Build, partner or buy external.
        • Handover & Interim Leadership — Running the setup until permanent leadership is in place.
      [Button] Scope a build

  03  AI Integration & Transformation
      AI in production, deployed. Not piloted.

      Where AI actually helps, and where it does not. Tools selected on what they do in production. Governance, rights and human sign-off defined, then tested on one live project before anything scales.

      Key focus areas
        • AI Workflow Mapping — Where AI genuinely speeds up or improves production, and where it doesn’t.
        • Tool & Vendor Evaluation — Independent assessment of AI production tools.
        • Governance & Rights Framework — Sign-off structures, usage rights and disclosure requirements for AI-generated content.
        • Human-in-the-Loop Design — Defining what stays human.
        • Pilot & Rollout Planning — Testing AI integration on one real production before scaling.
        • Team Enablement — Training and documentation for the people doing the work.
      [Button] Start with AI

  04  Production Controlling
      Cost is where strategy shows up. Read by people who know what things should cost.

      What a production should cost, established before the first quote arrives. Quotes reviewed line by line, then spend tracked as the production runs and final costs checked against what was scoped. Rates and buyouts benchmarked against what the market is paying now.

      Key focus areas
        • Cost Benchmarking — Production costs compared against real market rates.
        • Should-Cost Analysis — A cost estimate before the first quote arrives.
        • Bid Validation — Independent review of supplier quotes before they are signed.
        • Live Cost Tracking — Cost reporting throughout the production.
        • Buyout & Usage Rights — Term, territory and renewals, priced against actual use.
        • Invoice Reconciliation — Final costs checked against what was scoped and quoted.
      [Button] Pressure-test a budget

INDEPENDENT JUDGEMENT. ON YOUR SIDE.

THE KATA ARCHITECTURE INDEX
---------------------------
  Most production problems are operational. Every engagement is scored
  against the Index; a workshop or pilot goes deep on the one or two
  dimensions that matter most.

  01  Workflow & Process
      Most waste is invisible until someone maps it. We show you exactly where your time and budget leak between brief and delivery.
  02  Technology & Systems
      Is your tech stack earning its budget, or just adding complexity? We assess stack, integration and adoption to give you a straight answer.
  03  Budget & Resource
      Budget and reality rarely match by the end of a production. We examine structure, cost variance and governance to show you exactly where they diverge.
  04  Team & Capability
      Who decides what, and what happens when someone leaves? We map roles, decision rights and capability coverage to close the key-person risk.
  05  Pipeline & Quality
      Quality is made in specific places, and it erodes in specific places too. We trace content from brief to archive to find exactly where rework creeps in.
  06  Vendor & Partner
      Vendor rosters carry real variation in risk and value. We segment yours and assess contract structure to show which partners actually earn their place.

THE NAME
