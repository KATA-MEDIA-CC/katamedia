### 5 · The six dimensions (the "lenses")

These are the six things the audit scores (red / amber / green). Each has an icon, a number, a name, and a body. The **names are the IP** — reword only with care; the bodies are open to editing.

**01 · Workflow & Process**
We map the end-to-end flow from brief to delivery and quantify where time and money are lost. Most waste is invisible until someone maps it.

**02 · Technology & Systems**
We assess the tech stack, integration health and adoption: whether the investment is serving production goals or creating new complexity.

**03 · Budget & Resource**
We examine budget structure, cost-variance patterns and financial governance across the production lifecycle.

**04 · Team & Capability**
We map roles, decision rights, capability coverage and key-person risk. Who does what, who decides what, and what happens when someone leaves.

**05 · Pipeline & Quality**
We trace content from brief to archive and evaluate quality control, rework rates and feedback loops: where quality is made and where it erodes.

**06 · Vendor & Partner**
We segment the vendor portfolio and assess contract structure and service levels. Not all vendors are equal. Most brands treat them as if they are.

## Part 2 — Kata CI guide

The single source of truth for how Kata looks and reads. Don't improvise the palette, the type, or the voice.

### Palette

| Token | Value | Use |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Paper | `#F6F2E8` | Primary background (warm cream) |
| Paper-2 | `#EFEADB` | Secondary surface (bars, insets) |
| Paper-3 | `#E7E0CE` | Tertiary surface |
| Ink | `#1A1813` | Text, dark backgrounds (warm near-black) |
| Ink 80 / 60 / 40 / 25 | rgba tints of ink | Secondary text, captions, hairlines |
| Line | `rgba(26,24,19,.22)` | Structural borders, frames |
| Hair | `rgba(26,24,19,.12)` | Fine dividers |
| Clay | `#C0532C` | The one accent (burnt terracotta) |
| Clay-dark | `#A8451F` | Clay, pressed/darker; clay for small text on paper |

**Clay discipline.** Clay is for *display* only — the pivot word, section numbers, a single rule, the tick marks, `::selection`. **Never** clay for small body text or long runs of copy. Ink on paper does the reading; clay does the pointing. Paper is the default ground; ink grounds are for contrast moments only.

### Type

- **Serif — Spectral** (Georgia fallback): all body copy and editorial lines. Italic = emphasis, and italic-for-emphasis is always coloured **clay** (the pivot word).
- **Display / Sans — Schibsted Grotesk** (system-ui fallback): the wordmark, all headings, section numbers, and every label / kicker / caption / nav item.
- **Headings** are set **UPPERCASE**, weight 700 — the same big caps treatment across every section.
- **Labels** are uppercase, weight 600, wide tracking (`.14em`–`.24em`), small.
- Body reads in Spectral with generous line-height; the page breathes.

### The wordmark & the line

- The wordmark is **KATA**, uppercase, Schibsted Grotesk 600, tight tracking.
- **The line** — *Knowledge applied to action* — sits with the mark in **clay**, uppercase, justified to the mark's width. It appears on essentially every surface.
- **The four nevers:** don't stretch or condense the mark · don't recolour it (ink, or paper-on-dark — never clay) · don't add a second symbol · don't re-track the letters.
- Reversed = paper mark on ink; **the line stays clay.** Mono = all ink or all paper, no clay.

### Layout & structure

- Editorial, restrained, spacious. A warm paper ground, ink text, one clay accent.
- Hairline rules, **sharp 90° corners**, a subtle measuring / technical undertone.
- Numbering, eyebrows and ticks should encode something true (a real sequence, a real score) — never decoration.
- Section headings sit in the big caps style; a short serif deck sits under the heading.

### Voice

Terse, editorial, plain, confident. Short declaratives. No corporate hedging, no filler, no AI-tell phrasing.

The em-dash *is* used as an editorial device within the Kata voice ("From fragments — order"; "End to end or targeted — we do both"). This is a deliberate brand exception.

The identity's own lines, for register:
- "We sit beside the decision, not above the process."
- "Knowledge applied to action."
- "Independent judgement, applied in time."
- "From fragments — order."

Independence is the through-line: conflict-free, vendor-unaffiliated.
